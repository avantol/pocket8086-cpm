CP/M 2.2 material in this repository
====================================

src/cpm22.asm is Clark A. Calkins' 1981 reconstruction of the CP/M 2.2 CCP and
BDOS (from the "Unofficial CP/M Web Site", http://www.cpm.z80.de/), patched to
match Digital Research's distributed binary and re-origined for this system.

cpmfiles/*.COM are original Digital Research programs: the CP/M 2.2
utilities (ASM, DDT, DUMP, ED, LOAD, PIP, STAT, SUBMIT, XSUB) and DR's MAC,
RMAC, LINK, LIB and XREF, taken from the same site and from the CP/M 2.2
disk of the RomWBW project (https://github.com/wwarthen/RomWBW), whose
SUBMIT.COM includes DR's official patch.
